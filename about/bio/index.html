Bio

Olivia Seay grew up in Syracuse, New York. She earned her BA from Colorado College in 2005 and subsequently moved to the Pacific Northwest. In 2008, a volunteer art-teaching role brought her to South Africa’s South Coast and later Durban, where she lived on and off—including a brief stint studying in San Francisco in 2013—until moving back to the US in 2017.

Her return was difficult and marked a period of introspection and a trip to India, where establishing a meditation practice rekindled her lifelong passion for drawing and painting. In 2018, she attended a figure-drawing course in Italy led by a German artist. The following year, she relocated to Wiesbaden, Germany, to assist the artist with figure-drawing and portraiture courses. She visited Cape Town over the holidays, intending to resume the arrangement in Germany in the new year. However, the onset of the pandemic interrupted those plans, and she remained in South Africa. Olivia now maintains an independent painting practice from her home studio in Muizenberg, working primarily in oil on paper.
