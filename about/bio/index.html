Bio

Olivia Seay grew up in Syracuse, New York, and left in 2001 to pursue her undergraduate studies at Colorado College, graduating in 2005. She subsequently moved to the Pacific Northwest, where she lived until 2008, when she accepted a volunteer position teaching art in South Africa.

After volunteering on the South Coast, Olivia moved to Durban, where she lived until relocating to San Francisco to study in 2013. Later that year, she returned to Durban, where she remained until 2017, when she moved back to the United States. Her return was difficult and initiated a period of introspection, during which she established a meditation practice while on a trip to India. It was through meditation that she reconnected with her longstanding interest in drawing and painting.

In 2018, she attended a figure-drawing course in Italy led by a German artist. The following year, she relocated to Wiesbaden, Germany, to assist the artist with figure-drawing and portraiture courses. She visited Cape Town over the holiday break, intending to resume the arrangement in Germany in the new year. The onset of the COVID-19 pandemic interrupted those plans, and she remained in South Africa.

Olivia now maintains an independent painting practice from her home studio in Muizenberg, working primarily in oil on paper.
