About

Statement

My work emerges from a lifelong relationship with uncertainty. What began as an unconscious strategy for safety—remaining undecided, undefined, and therefore less vulnerable to rejection—created its own persistent state of discomfort.

I am interested now in what happens when that uncertainty is no longer something to escape or resolve, but a generative space: the in-between where opposing truths can coexist and reality is continually coming into being.

Bio

Olivia Seay is an American artist based in Cape Town, South Africa. Her current paintings on paper move between gesture, omission and the implicit body, exploring uncertainty as both tension and possibility.

She is also a certified mindfulness meditation teacher. Her practice is informed by attention, ambiguity and the capacity to hold opposing truths at once.
